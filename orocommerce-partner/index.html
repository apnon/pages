Why the partner matters more than the platform

OroCommerce is a good platform. It is also a deep one.

Price list rules, corporate account hierarchies, workflow, CPQ and multi-organization scale are exactly what make Oro right for a manufacturer or distributor, and exactly what a team learning it on your project will get wrong. Here is what working with a founding partner changes.

Platform depth

Price list rules, workflow definitions, account hierarchies and the API. We know which requirements Oro handles natively and which ones cost you a custom bundle, before you commit to a scope.

Integration

Before you choose anyone

Five questions worth asking every OroCommerce partner

Including us. If a partner cannot answer these quickly and specifically, that tells you something.

01

How many OroCommerce projects have you actually delivered?

Certification is a training course. Delivery is a different thing. Ask how many went live, and ask to speak to someone who ran one.

Our answerSynolia is one of OroCommerce's founding SI partners and has been building complex B2B commerce since 2004, for manufacturers and distributors in Europe, the US and Latin America.
02

Who is in the room when the ERP integration breaks?

Ask whether the people who scoped the project are the people who will build it, and where they sit. Integration problems are urgent and they need a real conversation, not a ticket.

Our answerThe team that scopes your project builds it, from Santiago, on your working day.
03

Can I see OroCommerce running before I sign anything?

Slides are cheap. Ask to click through a real B2B storefront with pricing, quotes and approvals, and ask how quickly they can show it on your own catalog.

Our answerOpen our live demo below right now, no signup. Or give us your catalog and we will build a personalized OroCommerce demo on your products and pricing within 24 hours.
04

What happens with local invoicing, tax and currency?

Electronic invoicing rules differ in every Latin American country. A partner who has not thought about it before the estimate will think about it during the build, at your cost.

Our answerLocal electronic invoicing, multi-currency and per-country tax handling are part of the scope from the first workshop.
05

What exactly is in the first release, and when does it go live?

A B2B commerce program that only delivers value in eighteen months usually delivers value never. Ask what is live in the first release, and what is deliberately left out.

Our answerWe scope a first release you can put in front of real customers, then build on it. We will tell you what to ignore for now.
