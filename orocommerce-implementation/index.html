What OroCommerce does natively, what needs configuration, what needs a custom bundle, and exactly which data moves between which systems in which direction. This is the document your estimate is built on.

8 to 16 weeks to first release

Scope discipline

What belongs in your first release, and what does not

The most useful thing a partner can tell you is what to leave out. Here is roughly how we split it, and we will argue about it with you honestly.

Release one
  • Corporate accounts and roles for your real customer structure.
  • Contract and tier pricing resolving correctly per customer.
  • Self-service ordering and reordering, the volume driver.
  • Live ERP integration for stock, prices, orders and invoices.
  • Compliant local invoicing in your launch country.
Deliberately later
  • Every country and every brand at once.
  • A full marketplace before the core store has users.
  • Perfect product data for the whole catalog.
  • Rebuilding every approval rule that exists on paper.
  • A redesign of the whole brand, mid-project.

FAQ

Implementation questions

How long does an OroCommerce implementation take?

A focused first release typically goes live in three to six months. The range depends almost entirely on integration: how clean your ERP data is, how many pricing rules you have, and how many systems have to agree with each other. A large multi-country rollout runs longer and should be phased.

Which ERPs do you integrate OroCommerce with?

SAP including S/4HANA and Business One, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle, Infor, Sage, Odoo, and Latin American systems such as Softland and Defontana, as well as in-house and legacy ERPs through their APIs, files or database views.

Do you handle Latin American electronic invoicing?

Yes. Electronic invoicing is country-specific: SII and DTE in Chile, CFDI and SAT in Mexico, DIAN in Colombia, SUNAT in Peru, and equivalent regimes elsewhere. We design the invoicing flow with your ERP and your local provider as part of the integration scope, not as a late surprise.

Can you migrate us from an existing platform?

Yes. We migrate catalogs, customer accounts, contract pricing, historic orders and content from Magento, Adobe Commerce, custom portals, and from the spreadsheets and PDF order forms that are often the real system. The hard part is rarely the data, it is agreeing what the pricing rules actually are.

Do you work fixed price or time and materials?

Both. We usually scope discovery and solution design as a fixed, short engagement, then run the build in increments. That way you get a real estimate based on your actual integration surface rather than a number invented before anyone looked at your ERP.
