Inherited someone else's build?

We take over OroCommerce projects. It starts with the truth.

Whether the previous partner left, the internal team moved on, or the project simply stalled, the first deliverable is an honest assessment, not a proposal.

What we usually find
  • Core files edited directly, so upgrades are blocked.
  • Custom code for things the platform already does natively.
  • Pricing logic reimplemented outside the price list engine.
  • Search and queue consumers left at default under real load.
  • No documented deployment path, and one person who knows it.
What you get from the audit
  • Version and upgrade path, with what blocks it and what it costs.
  • Customization inventory: keep, replace with native, or delete.
  • Integration health across ERP, PIM and CRM.
  • Performance findings against your real catalog and traffic.
  • A prioritized plan you own, whether or not you work with us.

FAQ

Developer and support questions

Can I hire OroCommerce developers as an extension of my own team?

Yes. Our developers can join your sprints, your board and your code review process, working as part of your team rather than behind a wall. Because we are based in Santiago de Chile, we overlap a full working day with Latin America and most of the US.

Can you take over an OroCommerce project someone else built?

Yes, and we do it regularly. We start with a technical audit: what version you are on, how far the code has diverged from the platform, what is custom that should not be, and what it will take to get you back to a supportable, upgradable state. You get that assessment as a document you own, whatever you decide afterwards.

Do you provide ongoing OroCommerce support and maintenance?

Yes. Support retainers cover incident response, security patches, platform upgrades, performance monitoring and a steady stream of small improvements, so your platform keeps moving between major projects.

How deep does your OroCommerce expertise go technically?

We work at platform level: custom Symfony bundles, the workflow engine, price list and price rule logic, extended entities, datagrids, ACL, the layout and Twig theming system, the JSON:API layer, the message queue, and Elasticsearch-backed website search.

What languages does your team work in?

Spanish, English and French. Daily technical work with Latin American teams runs in Spanish, and we work in English with US and international teams.
